Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated for the current CogniTrack release

This page explains, in plain terms, exactly what information CogniTrack collects and where it actually lives — and it's different depending on whether you're signed in or using the app as a guest.

What We Collect

When you fill out the User Information step, CogniTrack asks for your name, age, gender, education level, sleep quality, and (optionally) your dominant hand. As you move through the five assessment modules — Memory Recall, Focus & Attention, Decision Making, Thinking Speed, and Visual Reasoning — the app also records your responses, timing, and computed scores for each domain. If you register an account, we also store your email address and a securely hashed password — we never store your password in plain text and cannot look it up on your behalf.

Where Your Data Lives

As a guest, everything above stays in your browser's session storage — it is never transmitted to a server or written to a database, and is erased the moment you close the tab. As a signed-in user, your profile details and assessment results are saved to a PostgreSQL database, hosted on Supabase, against your account, so your dashboard and history are available whenever you log back in from any device. The application itself runs on Render, which serves every page over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Your account session is tracked with a secure, HTTP-only cookie used solely to keep you signed in — it is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

We keep signed-in account data for as long as your account exists. If you request deletion (see "Clearing Your Data" below), we permanently remove your profile and assessment history from our database — this cannot be undone.

Demo Mode

The "View Demo Dashboard" option loads a separate, clearly-labeled sample dataset (a fictional profile) so you can preview the dashboard experience before completing your own assessment. Demo data lives only in your browser's session storage and never mixes with, overwrites, or is mistaken for your real results.

Third-Party Resources

To render the interface, CogniTrack loads a small number of resources from third-party content delivery networks: Google Fonts (Inter and Poppins), Lucide Icons, and canvas-confetti. Requests to these providers may include standard request metadata such as your IP address, handled under each provider's own policies — independent of CogniTrack.

Clearing Your Data

  • As a guest, closing the browser tab or window clears your session immediately.
  • Starting a new assessment resets any prior in-progress attempt.
  • Signed-in users can request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time by emailing us below — we don't yet have a self-serve "delete my account" button, but every request is honored promptly.
