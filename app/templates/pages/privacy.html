Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated for the current CogniTrack prototype release

CogniTrack is a hackathon prototype — a browser-based cognitive assessment tool, not a finished commercial product. This page explains, in plain terms, exactly what information the app touches and — just as importantly — where that information actually lives.

What We Collect

When you fill out the User Information step, CogniTrack asks for your name, age, gender, education level, sleep quality, and (optionally) your dominant hand. As you move through the five assessment modules — Memory Recall, Focus & Attention, Decision Making, Thinking Speed, and Visual Reasoning — the app also records your responses, timing, and computed scores for each domain.

Where Your Data Lives

Everything above is stored exclusively in your browser's session storage. It is never transmitted to a server, never written to a database, and is automatically erased the moment you close the tab or browser window.

CogniTrack currently has no backend, no database, and no user accounts. There is nothing for anyone — including us — to access once your browser session ends.

Demo Mode

The "View Demo Dashboard" option loads a separate, clearly-labeled sample dataset (a fictional profile) so you can preview the dashboard experience before completing your own assessment. Demo data lives in its own storage namespace and never mixes with, overwrites, or is mistaken for your real results.

Third-Party Resources

To render the interface, CogniTrack loads a small number of resources from third-party content delivery networks: Google Fonts (Inter and Poppins), Lucide Icons, and canvas-confetti. Requests to these providers may include standard request metadata such as your IP address, handled under each provider's own policies — independent of CogniTrack.

Clearing Your Data

  • Closing the browser tab or window clears your session immediately.
  • Starting a new assessment from the User Information page resets any prior progress.
  • There is no "delete my account" control, because there is no account or server-side storage to delete from.

What's Changing Next

A future release will introduce optional accounts and persistent storage so you can save results across sessions. This policy will be updated well before that ships, and nothing described above will change retroactively without clear notice.
