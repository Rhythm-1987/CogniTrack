By using CogniTrack, you agree to the terms below. They're written to reflect what the app actually does today, not a hypothetical future version of it.

What CogniTrack Is

CogniTrack is a self-guided, five-domain cognitive assessment — Memory Recall, Focus & Attention, Decision Making, Thinking Speed, and Visual Reasoning — designed to give you a personal, informational snapshot of your cognitive performance in about fifteen minutes.

Not Medical Advice

CogniTrack is not a diagnostic, clinical, or medical tool. Your results should never be used to diagnose, treat, or make health decisions. If you have concerns about your cognitive health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Your Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information on the User Information step — age and other details factor into how your results are framed.
  • Try to complete the assessment in one sitting, in a reasonably distraction-free environment, for the most meaningful results.
  • Understand that results are estimates that can vary with attention, environment, fatigue, and the device you're using.

Data & Sessions

Your progress and results exist only for the duration of your browser session (see our Privacy Policy for details). Closing your browser, or starting a new assessment, clears that data. There is currently no way to save results long-term — that capability is planned for a future release alongside optional accounts.

Demo Mode

Content shown in Demo Mode is illustrative sample data, not a real assessment result, and must not be interpreted as reflecting your own performance.

No Warranty

CogniTrack is provided "as is" during active development. Features, scoring methodology, and result thresholds may change between releases without prior notice, and we make no guarantee of uninterrupted availability.
