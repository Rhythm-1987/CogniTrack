Answers to common questions about how CogniTrack works

Here are the questions people ask most about how CogniTrack works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No — you can take the full assessment as a guest by entering a few basic details on the Today's Assessment Check-In step. Creating a free account is optional, but it saves your results and history so you can come back to your dashboard from any device instead of losing progress when you close the tab.

Where is my data stored?

In the same database either way. As a guest, it's saved against a temporary, anonymous session identifier (not your name or an account) that's cleared when you fully close your browser. If you're signed in, your profile and results are saved against your account instead, so they're still there next time you log in. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

I refreshed my browser mid-assessment. Did I lose my progress?

No — refreshing during any module (Memory Recall, Focus & Attention, Decision Making, Thinking Speed, or Visual Reasoning) preserves where you left off in that module. If you navigate away first, the Assessment Hub's Resume Assessment button will pick you back up at the right module.

What does the Assessment Hub show if I haven't started yet?

A single Start Assessment button — nothing else. Once you begin a module, the hub switches to a Resume Assessment button instead, and after you finish all five it switches again to Start New Assessment and View Dashboard. You'll only ever see the one action that actually applies to where you are.

What is Demo Mode?

A sample dashboard preview built from fictional data, so you can see the reporting experience before completing your own assessment. It never affects, and is never affected by, your real results.

I closed my tab and lost my results — can I get them back?

If you were signed in, yes — log back in and open your Dashboard. If you took the assessment as a guest, just closing a tab is fine — your data is tied to your browser session, not the tab, so reopening the site in the same browser still shows it. Fully closing the browser (or clearing cookies) is what clears it for good, since it's never tied to an account — create one before your next attempt to keep results permanently and access them from any device.

Is this a medical assessment?

No. CogniTrack is for informational and self-insight purposes only — not a diagnostic or clinical tool. See our Terms of Use for details.

Does CogniTrack work on my phone or tablet?

Yes — every page is responsive and the assessments work on modern mobile browsers. For the reaction-time and timed modules, a larger screen and a physical keyboard can make responses slightly faster and more consistent, but nothing is blocked on mobile.

Do I need to install anything or create an internet connection mid-test?

No installation — CogniTrack runs entirely in your browser. You do need an internet connection to load the page initially (it pulls fonts and icons from a CDN), but once a page has loaded, the assessment logic itself runs locally.

Can I retake an assessment or take it more than once?

Yes. "Retake Assessment" on your dashboard clears your current session data and sends you back to Memory Recall for a fresh run. If you're signed in, each attempt is saved as its own entry in your Assessment History, so you can compare results over time — as a guest, only your most recent attempt is kept.

Can I download or print my results?

Not yet. "Download Report" is visible on the dashboard as a preview of what's coming, but it isn't enabled yet. You can still screenshot or print the dashboard page directly from your browser in the meantime.
